The same repository, one layer deeper

The generator that does not reject — it edits the candidate: the no-guess mode never throws a board away. It deforms the board at the exact spot where the guess would have been.

The arithmetic is sound — I went looking to break it and could not. What is not preserved is the distribution, and the documentation keeps its promise while meaning something else.
