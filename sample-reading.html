I read every file the project wrote itself (the parts of the page, the page header, the settings; the ready-made button and menu library that every Lovable project ships with I left out) and I opened the live site. I did not run the code. Where I say "on the live site", I checked it there; everything else comes from the files.

The short version. The site is in good shape for what it is: one page, no accounts, no database, no payments, nothing stored. That means very little can go badly wrong. What I found is about people who want to join and don't reach the club. Two things did that when I read it, and both are small fixes.

What's broken

  1. The main button may stop people at a Google login. The "join" button at the top opens a Google Form. From a browser that is not signed in to Google, it lands on a Google sign-in page, not on the form (checked on the live site). Most Android phones are signed in, so many visitors won't see this; anyone on a work laptop, an iPhone without a Google account, or a private window will. This usually comes from a setting in the form itself (for example "Limit to 1 response", which requires sign-in). Whoever owns the form can turn it off in a minute.
  2. The contact form says it was sent before anything is sent. When someone fills in the form at the bottom and presses the button, the site says the form was submitted, empties it, and opens WhatsApp with a ready message. Nothing reaches the coach until the visitor presses Send inside WhatsApp. If WhatsApp doesn't open (a laptop without it, a blocked new tab), or the visitor closes it, they believe they registered, their details are gone from the page, and the club never hears from them. Fix: say "Almost done: press Send in WhatsApp" instead, and don't empty the form.
  3. The phone field rejects ordinary phone numbers. It accepts exactly ten digits and nothing else. A parent who types the country code, or puts a space in the middle, gets cut off and sees an error. A number from another country can't be entered at all. Fix: accept spaces and the country code, and remove them before checking.
  4. The "Event" button in the menu does nothing. The event section was built to hide itself after a past training camp, which is a good idea. The menu button stayed. Clicking it on the live site leaves you where you were. Fix: remove the button, or put the next event in the section.

What looks wrong to a visitor

  1. When someone shares the site, the preview shows Lovable, not the club. On WhatsApp, Facebook or X, the picture in the preview is Lovable's own image, and the text under it is a placeholder line, not a description. The page also names Lovable as its author. For a club that grows by parents sending the link to other parents, this preview is the first thing most people see. Fix: a club photo and one real sentence (who, where, what age).
  2. Two numbers that disagree. Two parts of the page give the coach's years of experience, and they don't match. A parent comparing clubs notices that.
  3. One schedule block is unclear. Two blocks in the schedule share the same address; one is called "Extra Session", and a note in the code gives it a different name. If it is a kids' class or a different place, the page should say so.
  4. The "Edit with Lovable" badge shows in the corner of the live site. Some owners don't mind; whether it shows is decided in the Lovable account, not in the code.

What's heavy

  1. The coach's photo is a 2 MB file shown at the size of a passport photo. The six photos at the top all load at once (about 1.6 MB together), and an animated 3D background runs the whole time someone is on the page. The site's own program is 855 KB before any photo, and the 3D library is part of it. I did not measure loading time, so I won't claim the site is slow; these are file sizes, and on a weak mobile connection they are what makes the first screen wait. Fix: save the photo at its real size (a few dozen KB), and ask whether the 3D background earns its cost on phones.

What's risky

Very little, because the site keeps nothing. Two things worth a thought:

  • The gallery shows many children. That is normal for a club like this; just make sure the parents agreed to the photos being on a public website.
  • The code is public on GitHub. Nothing secret was in it when I read it (the phone numbers in it are already on the site). Keep it that way if a login or payments are ever added.

Smaller things

The menu button on phones has no label, so a screen reader announces only "button". The addresses are plain text; an "open in Maps" link next to each would help. The phone number at the top of the page can't be tapped to call (the one in the contact section can).

What to do first

  1. Open the Google Form's settings and make sure it opens without signing in. (Minutes.)
  2. Change the "submitted" message and stop emptying the form. (Minutes, for whoever edits the site.)
  3. Remove the "Event" button, or add the next event.
  4. Fix the link preview: a club photo and a real sentence.
  5. Let the phone field accept spaces and the country code.

Everything else can wait.
